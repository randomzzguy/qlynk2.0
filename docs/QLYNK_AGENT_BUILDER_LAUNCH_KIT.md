# Qlynk Agent Builder Launch Kit

## Positioning

### Freelancer promise

Build a professional Qlynk Agent for a client without inventing a new discovery process, prompt, or quality checklist for every project.

### Business promise

Hire a freelancer to turn your approved business information into a focused Qlynk Agent that you own, review, and maintain.

### Honest boundary

The workflow improves consistency and catches missing information. It does not guarantee perfect answers, automatic income, or autonomous business operations.

## The service freelancers can sell

### Offer name

**Custom Qlynk Agent Setup**

### Core deliverables

- Client discovery interview
- Source and permission review
- Agent identity, purpose, audience, scope, and tone
- Approved facts, FAQs, documents, and website sources
- Missing-information and human-handoff behavior
- A minimum 12-question launch test
- Public-page or website-widget setup
- Client training and ownership handoff

### Optional recurring service

**Qlynk Agent Maintenance**

- Review knowledge gaps and repeated questions
- Update approved prices, services, products, policies, and availability
- Re-index changed website sources
- Remove obsolete information
- Retest affected answers and boundaries
- Provide a monthly or quarterly maintenance summary

### Scope before quoting

Ask the freelancer to price based on:

- number and quality of source documents;
- number of products, services, locations, or audiences;
- whether policies or regulated information require extra review;
- number of facts and FAQs to enter;
- whether a website widget must be installed;
- whether copywriting, source cleanup, or recurring maintenance is included.

Keep the freelancer's project fee separate from the client's Qlynk subscription.

- privacy, terms, website, repository, and support details;
- three starter prompts;

- confirm the publisher's verified developer or business identity;
- confirm Apps Management write access;
- capture final plugin screenshots if requested by the submission surface;

- submit through the plugin portal and wait for review.
